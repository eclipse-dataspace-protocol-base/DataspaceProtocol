# Project Status and Standardisation Notice

This repository contains the Dataspace Protocol specification and related artifacts. The project is actively maintained and moving toward formal standardisation via submission as a PAS (Publicly Available Specification) to ISO/IEC JTC 1. The purpose of this notice is to explain the rationale for the PAS submission, provide current status information, outline the procedure that will be followed, and describe next steps and where to discuss updates.

## Justification for PAS Submission to ISO/IEC JTC

- **Interoperability and reliability:** Formalising the specification via ISO/IEC will help ensure broader, global interoperability and increase reliability among implementers and adopters.
- **Industry alignment:** A PAS submission accelerates recognition of the specification as a stable, industry-ready baseline while allowing the community to retain control over the evolution of the specification.
- **Faster route to adoption:** The PAS route is designed to enable faster publication and uptake than a full international standard, which is important for technologies that require timely adoption.

## Current Status
- The specification and supporting materials are in active development within this repository.
- The [release 2025-1](https://github.com/eclipse-dataspace-protocol-base/DataspaceProtocol/releases/tag/2025-1-err1) has been approved by the Eclipse Foundation and the Eclipse Dataspace Working Group. 
- This release has been sent as PAS Submission to the ISO/IEC secretariat. An editorial review is conducted before the DIS ballot starts. (See the details of the procedure [here]() ) 
- The Eclipse Dataspace Protocol team follows the common practice in ISO/IEC standardization to not work on documents while they are under ballot (stand-still).

## Procedure to Be Followed
1. Finalise the candidate specification version and any companion documentation (change logs, test vectors, examples, and normative references). (Done :white_check_mark:)
2. Run a community review and comment period within the repository and linked community channels; incorporate agreed changes. (Done :white_check_mark:)
3. Prepare the PAS submission package in accordance with the internal regulations and with the support of our PAS mentor. (Done :white_check_mark:)
4. Submit the PAS package to ISO/IEC JTC via the Eclipse Foundation. (Done :white_check_mark:)
5. Respond to any comments from ISO/IEC review and, if necessary, update the specification for publication. 

## Next Steps
- The Eclipse Dataspace Protocol is published as [ISO/IEC DIS 26450](https://www.iso.org/standard/93502.html). 
- The team is waiting for the DIS ballot to be closed. 
- Once the ballot is closed and the document is approved without technical comments, it may be published as ISO/IEC standard
- Comments received during the ballot will be processed by the team accordingly. If required the specification will be resubmitted. 
- The team will work on open and new issues on the specification when the ballot is closed and will decide then, if the standard needs to be updated or ammended.
